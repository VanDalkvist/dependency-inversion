## Impulse mark: canonical selected artwork

- SVG: `public/aha-impulse-mark.svg` (light), `public/aha-impulse-mark-dark.svg` (dark), `public/favicon.svg` (small-size sign).
- Form starts from the official Vanya Arc mark path `M 97 293 … 595 227`, then adds three **short separated rays** above the end of the warm crest. **No** top orbit and **no** additional dot: those belong to rejected variants.
- Wordmark reads **Vanya Arc / Aha!**; *Vanya Arc* uses normal ink color, slash secondary, *Aha!* accent terracotta. At very narrow widths, only the subbrand wordmark and sign remain visible.
- Do not loop or pulse the rays continuously; any future motion must be brief and meaningful, with reduced-motion support.
